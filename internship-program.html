Blue Skate Presents

Youth Internship Program

Building Skills. Developing Leaders. Preparing Young People for Life.

The Blue Skate Youth Internship Program gives young people hands-on experience while helping them develop leadership, communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and career-readiness skills.

Program Benefits

Participants Have the Opportunity To:

  • Develop leadership and communication skills
  • Build confidence, responsibility, and accountability
  • Gain hands-on work and community experience
  • Strengthen teamwork and problem-solving skills
  • Learn goal-setting and positive decision-making
  • Receive guidance and support from Blue Skate Life Coaches
  • Explore career and leadership opportunities
  • Develop skills that can benefit them in school, work, and everyday life

Leadership & Personal Growth

Through leadership activities and personal-development reading, interns explore books including:

Who Moved My Cheese? for Teens by Dr. Spencer Johnson, The Four Agreements by Don Miguel Ruiz, and Leadership 101 by John C. Maxwell.

“At Blue Skate, we are not just preparing young people for careers — we are helping prepare them for life.”
