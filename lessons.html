Our Mission

(What is Blue Skate?)

Blue Skate, LLC is an organization that teaches roller skating to all age groups. The art of skating teaches students to work together to improve their physical, mental, social, and emotional development through physical movement.

Blue Skate's mission is to give back to the community from a roller-skating perspective: sharing knowledge, wisdom and love to our youth, at the same time building strong, active, lifelong learners. We are dedicated to shaping lives by promoting physical and mental health, and hygiene. Our focus is on the safety and well-being of all, whether it is teaching how to get up gracefully after every fall, or in life.

Our purpose is to ensure that students work safely and collaboratively while learning the academic vocabulary and physical movements of roller skating. Students build interpersonal and communication skills to work in partnerships and groups, while learning to effectively follow directions.

Our Programs

Four ways Blue Skate shows up for skaters and schools across all 5 boroughs.

Incentives Program for NYCDOE

We partner with NYCDOE, charter, and private schools to offer incentive and after-school skating programs across all 5 boroughs that encourage attendance, participation, and school success.

Community Skating Events

Family-friendly skating events that bring neighbors together on wheels, building community one roll at a time.

High Schools for NYCDOE and Charters

Hands-on learning, skill-building, and real-world experience for high school students across all 5 boroughs through our internship program with NYCDOE, charter, and private schools.

Motivational Books

Motivational books that pair with our skate lessons — building resilience and a growth mindset both on and off the rink.

Community Partner: Kiwanis Kings Plaza Canarsie

David Isaac serves as Web Master Chair for Kiwanis Kings Plaza Canarsie, a chapter of Kiwanis International dedicated to serving children and communities through service, incentives, and life coaching — values that carry directly into every Blue Skate lesson and program.
